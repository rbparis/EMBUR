# EMBUR Version 3

## Product promise

EMBUR is a coordinated workforce for independent service businesses. Each agent has one job, shares what it learns, and is measured against calls, booked work, repeat business, referrals, and revenue.

## Agent divisions

1. **Call Agent** — answers, reassures, qualifies, and escalates.
2. **Booking Agent** — converts customer need into a scheduled job.
3. **Follow-Up Agent** — reopens estimates and unfinished opportunities.
4. **Relationship Agent** — handles thank-yous, milestones, weather care, and repeat-business moments.
5. **Referral Agent** — asks for introductions when customer trust is highest.
6. **Reputation Agent** — requests reviews and turns strong work into proof.
7. **Video Agent** — builds short product, service, educational, and advertising videos.
8. **Traffic Agent** — researches demand, runs owner-approved campaigns, and measures booked revenue.
9. **Atlas** — directs the workforce, shares context, and improves each agent from outcomes.

## Three engines

1. **Capture** — after-hours intake, missed opportunities, customer context, priority, and follow-up.
2. **Relationships** — permission-based weather care, birthdays, holidays, thank-yous, service anniversaries, and seasonal reactivation.
3. **Growth** — campaign planning, creative, owner education, referral tracking, paid advertising, and revenue attribution.

## Plans

- **Copper — $99/month:** capture and daily operating clarity.
- **Silver — $199/month:** automated follow-up and relationship care.
- **Gold — $399/month:** campaigns, reactivation, analytics, and recurring referral tracking.
- **Diamond — $799/month:** specialized Atlas Growth Agents and owner-approved paid campaign management. Advertising spend is separate.

## Trust rules

- Customer consent and communication preferences are respected.
- Atlas drafts and recommends; the owner controls sends and automation permissions.
- No paid campaign launches and no budget changes occur without owner approval.
- Marketing performance is measured against leads, bookings, and revenue rather than vanity metrics.
- Partner payouts require published eligibility, attribution, tax, payout, and anti-abuse terms.
